Privacy

Miji collects no data. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, and that is a description of how the app is built rather than a policy it follows.

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
  • No servers. Miji has no backend. The whole dictionary ships inside the app, so look-ups work offline and never leave the phone.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers. Nothing in the app reports usage anywhere.
  • Your words stay on your device. Saved words, flashcards and review history are stored on your iPhone, in the app's own container, which is shared only with Miji's Share Sheet and notification extensions on the same phone.
  • Notifications are local. Review prompts are scheduled by the app on the device itself, not sent from a server.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything. There is no copy anywhere else.

If Miji ever offers optional iCloud sync, it will use your own iCloud account and be off until you turn it on; this page will say so first.

Questions: open an issue or discussion at the Miji site.
